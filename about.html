A Message from Our CEO

As the CEO and founder of Zodiac Collection, I'm thrilled to share our brand's story with you. Growing up, I've always been fascinated by the mysteries of the universe and the unique characteristics of each zodiac sign. As a Gemini myself, I'm drawn to the versatility and creativity that comes with being a twin sign. This fascination sparked a passion to create a fashion brand that combines the magic of the cosmos with high-end style.


Emmil Bamf.

OUR MISSION

At BAMF Enterprises, we're committed to redefining sports fashion by creating jerseys that transcend the boundaries between athletic wear and high fashion. We believe that everyone deserves to feel bold and magnificent in what they wear.

Each collection is limited to maintain exclusivity and quality. We don't mass produce - we carefully craft each piece to ensure it meets our exacting standards.
